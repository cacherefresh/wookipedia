## Fitness

### Strength Training
- Benefits: increases muscle mass, supports bone density, improves metabolism  
- Examples: weightlifting, bodyweight exercises, resistance bands  
- Safety: start light, use proper form, rest between sessions  

### Cardio
- Benefits: supports cardiovascular health, burns calories, improves endurance  
- Examples: running, cycling, swimming, jump rope  
- Safety: pace yourself, hydrate, mix high- and low-intensity workouts  

### Flexibility
- Benefits: improves range of motion, reduces injury risk, enhances recovery  
- Examples: stretching, yoga, pilates  
- Safety: warm up first, avoid overextending joints  

### Recovery
- Benefits: allows muscle repair, prevents overtraining, supports mental wellbeing  
- Examples: rest days, massage, foam rolling, proper sleep  
- Safety: listen to your body, adapt intensity if sore or fatigued  

### Mental Fitness
- Benefits: builds resilience, reduces stress, supports focus and emotional balance  
- Examples: meditation, breathing exercises, journaling, visualization  
- Safety: practice consistently, start with short sessions, seek professional guidance if severe anxiety or trauma is involved

# SUPPLEMENTS

## Shilajit
**Description:** A tar-like resin found in Himalayan rocks, rich in fulvic acid and minerals.  

**Benefits:**  
- Detoxification & Chelation: binds heavy metals and supports liver/kidney detox  
- Neuroprotection: antioxidant effects, supports mitochondrial function, may help in Parkinson’s/Alzheimer’s  
- Energy & Vitality: enhances ATP production, reduces fatigue  
- Cognitive Support: improves memory and focus  

**Safety:**  
- Only use purified shilajit; raw resin may contain contaminants  
- Avoid with hemochromatosis or during pregnancy  
- Possible interactions with blood pressure or diabetes meds  

---

## Ashwagandha
**Description:** An adaptogenic herb from Ayurveda, also known as *Withania somnifera.*  

**Benefits:**  
- Stress Reduction: lowers cortisol and anxiety  
- Energy & Stamina: supports endurance and recovery  
- Cognitive Support: may enhance focus and memory  
- Hormonal Balance: supports thyroid and testosterone levels  

**Safety:**  
- Generally safe in moderate doses  
- Avoid in pregnancy  
- May interact with sedatives, thyroid meds  

---

## Rhodiola
**Description:** An adaptogenic root used in traditional medicine of Russia and Scandinavia.  

**Benefits:**  
- Stress Adaptation: improves resilience to fatigue  
- Cognitive Support: enhances focus, attention, and mental energy  
- Mood Regulation: may reduce mild depression and anxiety  
- Physical Endurance: supports stamina and recovery  

**Safety:**  
- Safe short-term, long-term data limited  
- May overstimulate if combined with caffeine  
- Not recommended in bipolar disorder

# GLOSSARY

## TERMS

### Chelation
The process by which compounds (like fulvic acid) bind to heavy metals and help remove them from the body.  

### Adaptogen
A natural substance that helps the body resist stress and maintain balance without disrupting normal function.  

### Neuroprotection
Strategies or substances that preserve the structure and function of nerve cells against injury, toxins, or degeneration.  

### Fulvic Acid
A major active component in shilajit with antioxidant and detoxification properties.  

### Abstinence-Only Education
A prevention approach that discourages any sexual activity until marriage; criticized for being ineffective compared to harm-reduction education.  

### Harm Reduction
A set of practical strategies to reduce the negative consequences of risky behaviors, such as drug use, without demanding complete abstinence.
