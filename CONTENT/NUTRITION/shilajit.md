## NAME  
**shilajit** — natural resin supplement used in Ayurveda with potential detoxification and neuroprotective effects  

---
## SYNOPSIS  
Optional flags:  
- `--purified` — ensure processed/purified resin only  
- `--consult` — check with healthcare provider before use  

---

## DESCRIPTION  
**Shilajit** is a tar-like resin found in mountain ranges such as the Himalayas.  
It is composed primarily of **fulvic acid**, **dibenzo-α-pyrones**, and a wide spectrum of **trace minerals**.  

Traditionally used in **Ayurvedic medicine** as a rejuvenator, vitality enhancer, and adaptogen.  

---

## BENEFITS  

### Detoxification & Chelation  
- Fulvic acid binds toxic metals (lead, mercury, cadmium).  
- Supports liver and kidney detoxification.  

### Neuroprotection  
- Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties.  
- May slow tau protein buildup (Alzheimer’s).  
- Mitochondrial support may benefit Parkinson’s disease.  

### Energy & Vitality  
- Enhances ATP production.  
- Reduces fatigue and improves endurance.  

### Cognitive Function  
- Supports memory and focus.  
- May influence dopamine regulation for mood and motor function.  

### Other Benefits  
- May support testosterone and reproductive health.  
- Promotes bone and joint strength.  
- Acts as an adaptogen for stress management.  

---

## COMPOSITION  
- **Fulvic acid** (antioxidant, chelation)  
- **Dibenzo-α-pyrones** (neuroprotective)  
- **Trace minerals** (iron, zinc, magnesium, selenium, copper, etc.)  

---

## SAFETY  
- Always use **purified** shilajit; raw resin may contain harmful contaminants.  
- Avoid if pregnant, breastfeeding, or with **hemochromatosis** (iron overload).  
- Possible interactions: blood pressure medications, diabetes drugs, hormone therapy.  

---

## RESEARCH  
- Evidence primarily from preclinical and small human studies.  
- Promising results for **Alzheimer’s** and **Parkinson’s**, but requires larger clinical trials.
